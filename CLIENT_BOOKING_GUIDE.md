# How Clients Access Booking Tracking 📱

## Where to Find Your Bookings:

### Step 1: Go to Profile
1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar (👤)
2. You'll see your profile screen

### Step 2: Open Bookings Tab
1. Swipe or tap on the **"Bookings"** tab
2. You'll see a list of all your bookings

### Step 3: View Booking Details
1. Tap on any booking card
2. The **Booking Tracking Screen** opens with:
   - ✅ Live map showing artisan location
   - ✅ Your location marker
   - ✅ Animated route between you
   - ✅ Real-time distance
   - ✅ Message & Call buttons
   - ✅ QR code for payment release

## Navigation Flow:

```
Bottom Bar → Profile (👤)
    ↓
Profile Screen → Bookings Tab
    ↓
Bookings List → Tap a booking
    ↓
Booking Tracking Screen (Live Map!)
```

## Tabs in Profile:

1. **About** - Your bio and info
2. **Portfolio** - Your posts (if artisan)
3. **Reviews** - Reviews you've received
4. **Services** - Services you offer (if artisan)
5. **Bookings** ← HERE! Your booking history
6. **Saved** - Saved posts

## Booking Card Shows:

- Service title
- Artisan name
- Date & time
- Status (Pending/Confirmed/In Progress/Completed)
- Price
- **"Track Booking"** button (if active)

## When Can You Track?

You can track bookings when status is:
- ✅ **Confirmed** - Artisan accepted
- ✅ **In Progress** - Service is happening
- ❌ **Pending** - Waiting for artisan to accept
- ❌ **Completed** - Service finished
- ❌ **Cancelled** - Booking cancelled

## What You See in Tracking:

### Map View:
- 🟢 **Green marker** - Your location (client)
- 🔵 **Blue marker** - Artisan location
- 📍 **Blue route** - Path from artisan to you
- 📏 **Distance** - Real-time distance in km

### Actions:
- 💬 **Message** - Chat with artisan
- 📞 **Call** - Phone call
- 🗺️ **Get Directions** - Opens Google Maps
- 📱 **Show QR** - For payment release

### Info:
- Service details
- Scheduled date & time
- Your address (resolved from GPS)
- Artisan & client profiles

## Status Colors:

- 🟡 **Yellow** - Pending
- 🔵 **Blue** - Confirmed
- 🟠 **Orange** - In Progress
- 🟢 **Green** - Completed
- 🔴 **Red** - Cancelled/Failed

---

**TL;DR**: Profile → Bookings Tab → Tap booking → See live map! 🗺️
